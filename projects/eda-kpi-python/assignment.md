# Customer Churn Analysis — Task Assignment

> **Portfolio version:** This assignment has been adapted and translated into English for public presentation. Original company branding and identifying information have been removed or generalized.

## Problem Statement

A subscription-based fitness application has experienced an increase in customer churn over the past two quarters. As customer acquisition costs rise, retaining existing subscribers has become a key business priority.

The objective is to explore customer account information and user activity to identify patterns associated with churn, define meaningful business KPIs, and recommend practical actions to improve customer retention.

## Objectives

- Validate and prepare the provided datasets for analysis.
- Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) to investigate customer churn and its relationship with customer engagement and subscription plan type.
- Create visualizations to communicate key patterns and differences between customer groups.
- Define relevant business KPIs based on the analytical findings and estimate their baseline values.
- Translate findings into actionable recommendations for improving customer retention.
- Present the results in a concise, business-oriented slide presentation for a non-technical audience.

## Dataset

The analysis uses the following datasets:

**Account Information**
- `customer_id` — unique customer identifier
- `email` — customer email address
- `state` — customer state
- `plan` — subscription plan type
- `plan_list_price` — listed subscription price
- `churn` — indicator of whether the customer has churned

**User Activity**
- `event_time` — timestamp of the activity event
- `user_id` — customer identifier
- `event_type` — type of user activity

## Deliverables

- **Analytical solution:** Data validation, preprocessing, exploratory analysis, and KPI calculations using an appropriate technology of choice.
- **Business presentation:** A concise slide deck presenting the business problem, analytical approach, key findings, baseline KPIs, and practical retention recommendations for a non-technical leadership audience.
