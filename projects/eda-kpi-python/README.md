# EDA & Business KPIs — Python

## Problem Statement
Analyze customer churn for a subscription-based fitness application to identify patterns related to customer retention and support data-driven business decisions.

## Objectives
- Validate and preprocess customer account and activity data.
- Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) to investigate churn patterns.
- Analyze relationships between customer engagement, subscription plans, and churn.
- Define relevant business KPIs and estimate baseline values.
- Provide actionable recommendations to improve customer retention.
- Present key findings and recommendations in a concise business presentation.
